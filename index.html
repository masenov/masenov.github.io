Martin Asenov
Hi, I'm Martin, a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Parexel AI Labs.
I enjoy turning novel research ideas into production-grade systems — currently working on document intelligence (RAG, OCR and vision-language models) for corpus-level QA and large-scale unstructured document understanding in clinical trials.

Previously I led ML systems research at Huawei R&D UK across LLM inference, time-series foundation models, and accelerator-aware deployment, with publications at ICML, ICLR, EuroSys, SoCC, EuroMLSys and IC2E. Before that I was at Hyperscience and interned with Amazon Prime Air and DSTL.

I did my PhD at The University of Edinburgh and Heriot-Watt University in the RAD group, working at the intersection of robotics, machine learning and physics. I also spent a year as an exchange student at UC Irvine.

Always open to interesting projects — feel free to reach out.
